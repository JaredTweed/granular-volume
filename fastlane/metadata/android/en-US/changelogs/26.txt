Volume keys now climb smoothly out of the quiet zone: once your presses have
eased the extra attenuation away, further presses raise the volume normally
instead of being pulled back down.

If something keeps raising the volume while the quiet zone is protecting it,
a second raise by you now wins immediately.

During a regular phone call the quiet steps are shown dimmed, because Android
routes call audio through a path that carries no audio effects and nothing can
lower it further. App calls such as WhatsApp are unaffected and still use the
full range.
