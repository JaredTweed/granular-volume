Volume keys now climb smoothly out of the quiet zone: once your presses have
eased the extra attenuation away, further presses raise the volume normally
instead of being pulled back down.

If something else keeps raising the volume, a second raise by you now wins.

On a regular phone call the quiet steps are shown dimmed. Android carries call
audio on a path that runs no audio effects. App calls such as WhatsApp keep the
full range on most phones.
