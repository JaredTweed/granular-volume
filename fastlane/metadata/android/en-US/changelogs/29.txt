Seven days of everything, then one payment, once. New installs get the complete
app for seven days, after that it asks for the Full Range Key, a one-time purchase
on Google Play. No subscription, nothing that renews.

Already using the app? Nothing changes for you, permanently. Every step you had
stays yours.

Also in this release: volume keys climb smoothly out of the quiet zone, a second
raise by you wins over anything else raising the volume, and on a regular phone
call the quiet steps are shown dimmed, because Android carries call audio on a
path that runs no audio effects. App calls such as WhatsApp keep the full range
on most phones.
